# Code of Conduct

## Our expectations

Everyone participating in AIDRAX OS issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces should be treated with respect, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

- Be welcoming, patient, and considerate.
- Give constructive feedback about the work.
- Respect different viewpoints and accept reasonable corrections.
- Respect privacy and keep discussions relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks, spam, and publishing another person's private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and moderation

For a public incident, use GitHub's **Report content** action where available. You may also ask the maintainer, [@Nitram1984](https://github.com/Nitram1984), for moderation on the affected thread, without repeating abusive material or disclosing private information.

For sensitive incidents, use GitHub's private reporting tools rather than posting personal details publicly. Security vulnerabilities must follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.md).

The maintainer may edit or remove inappropriate content, lock discussions, or restrict participation as appropriate to the severity and repetition of the behavior. Moderation should be fair, proportionate, and respectful of privacy.

This code applies to participation in project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
